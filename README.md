# AI_EPC_ENGINE 🏗️⚡

**AI Intelligence Platform for Data Centre EPC Project Delivery**
*Built for the ET AI Hackathon 2026*

## 📖 Overview

India is experiencing a massive data centre construction boom, but EPC (Engineering, Procurement, and Construction) projects face significant challenges, with 67% experiencing schedule overruns exceeding 10%. Information fragmentation across specifications, submittals, test records, and RFIs leads to commissioning failures and delays.

**AI_EPC_ENGINE** is an AI-powered EPC Project Intelligence platform that unifies project documents, specifications, schedules, procurement data, and quality records into a living intelligence layer. It enables proactive schedule management, automated compliance and quality checking, and real-time commissioning support across the full project lifecycle to ensure Tier III and IV certifications.

## ✨ Core Features

*   **Specification & Quality Compliance Agent:** Automatically ingests and checks equipment specifications, design standards, and client requirements against procurement orders and vendor submittals, flagging deviations before they reach the site.
*   **Predictive Schedule Risk Engine:** A multi-agent system analyzing real-time procurement status, equipment lead times, workforce availability, and weather to identify critical path risks weeks in advance and generate mitigation options.
*   **Supply Chain Visibility & Risk Agent:** Tracks critical equipment shipments (UPS systems, generators, cooling towers) across multi-tier suppliers to alert teams of at-risk deliveries and model procurement alternatives.
*   **Commissioning Quality Assurance Copilot:** An AI agent trained on data centre commissioning standards (TIA-942, Uptime Institute) that guides engineers through integrated testing sequences, flags non-conformances, and auto-generates test records.
*   **Project Knowledge & RFI Intelligence Agent:** A RAG-powered conversational layer over all project documents that answers technical and contractual queries in seconds, citing sources and identifying previously resolved RFIs.

## 🛠️ Proposed Tech Stack

*   **Agentic AI & Multi-Agent Systems**
*   **RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)** for technical specifications and RFI logs
*   **Computer Vision** for automated drawing review and submittal checking
*   **Geospatial Intelligence** for tracking supply chain shipments
*   **Predictive Analytics** for schedule and cost risk modeling
