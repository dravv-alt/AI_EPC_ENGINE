# Pramana Cx — Final Demonstration Video Script

**Recommended duration:** 6–7 minutes  
**Audience:** Project judges and evaluators  
**Project:** Pramana Cx — Evidence Control Plane for EPC Commissioning

## Before recording

- Use the seeded **Mumbai DC-07** project.
- Start on the **Overview** page.
- Keep browser zoom at 100%, hide bookmarks, and use full-screen mode.
- Preload the key pages: Overview, Sources, Graph, Cx Tests, Compliance, Schedule, Shipments, Command Center, Readiness, and Turnover.
- Speak slowly and demonstrate one continuous commissioning story rather than every available button.

## Demonstration script

| Time | Screen / action | Narration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:25 | **Overview dashboard** | “Good morning. We are presenting Pramana Cx, an evidence control plane for mission-critical EPC commissioning. The core problem we address is that commissioning decisions are usually spread across documents, test reports, schedules, emails, and field observations. Pramana Cx brings those records into one governed workflow, so every readiness decision is traceable and defensible.” |
| 0:25–0:45 | Stay on Overview; point to readiness, evidence, actions, and alerts | “Our key design principle is simple: AI can assist with extraction, retrieval, drafting, and recommendations—but it never makes an authoritative decision. Readiness, schedules, and test verdicts are calculated deterministically, while final acceptance remains with an authorized engineer.” |
| 0:45–1:10 | Overview cards and **Gate Progress** panel | “This is the project control room. It gives the team an immediate view of gate readiness, accepted evidence, open actions, active alerts, delivery status, and the progress of every commissioning gate. Instead of asking, ‘Where are we blocked?’, the team can see the answer immediately.” |
| 1:10–1:40 | Open **Sources** | “Every governed decision starts with controlled source material. In Sources, users upload immutable project documents such as procedures, standards, drawings, and specifications. Each revision is tracked, and the system extracts traceable citation regions from the controlled source.” |
| 1:40–2:05 | Point to source revision/status and page chips | “This ensures that a requirement, compliance comparison, or checklist step is never based on an unclear document reference. It can always be traced back to the exact controlled revision and extracted page region.” |
| 2:05–2:35 | Open **Graph & Timeline** | “The Graph and Timeline view shows how the project’s controlled records connect. Here we can trace relationships between assets, commissioning gates, requirements, evidence, and audit events. For example, selecting a gate immediately reveals its preceding gates, linked requirements, and related project records.” |
| 2:35–2:55 | Select or point at a gate and its neighbours | “This is important because commissioning is not a collection of isolated tasks. Every decision has upstream dependencies and downstream consequences, and this view makes that context visible.” |
| 2:55–3:25 | Open **Cx Tests** | “Next is the commissioning workflow. Pramana Cx can ingest controlled standards and procedures, then generate citation-grounded draft checklists. Engineers still select the applicable system, gate, equipment, and governing standards.” |
| 3:25–3:45 | Point to completed standards and cited draft controls | “For test execution, numeric and boolean readings receive deterministic verdicts. Narrative observations are deliberately routed to human review. After approval, a test report becomes immutable evidence instead of an editable standalone document.” |
| 3:45–4:10 | Open **Compliance** | “The Compliance Workbench compares accepted requirements with exact controlled target lines from submittals, purchase orders, drawings, or other project documents. It can identify candidate deviations, but it does not auto-accept them.” |
| 4:10–4:25 | Point at comparison selectors and review queue | “This gives engineers a faster review process while preserving their authority over compliance disposition.” |
| 4:25–4:55 | Open **Schedule**, Current Schedule | “For planning, Pramana Cx uses reviewed project inputs to create immutable schedule versions. The current schedule highlights task sequencing, critical-path activities, and the deterministic planning result.” |
| 4:55–5:15 | Show risk-mitigation cards or schedule risks | “The platform also monitors procurement, workforce, weather, and equipment risks. It can propose mitigation options, but these remain advisory. No AI-generated risk or mitigation can directly alter the schedule or project dates.” |
| 5:15–5:40 | Open **Shipments** | “For supply-chain visibility, the Shipment Navigator combines route monitoring, ETA context, weather overlays, congestion signals, and shipment status in one view. This connects material movement directly to commissioning planning.” |
| 5:40–6:00 | Open **Command Center** | “The Command Center consolidates operational alerts. Each alert is connected to its exact affected context—whether that is a task, gate, risk, finding, or shipment—so the team does not need to manually search across separate tools.” |
| 6:00–6:30 | Open **Readiness** | “This is the readiness decision view. For every commissioning gate, Pramana Cx shows accepted proof, missing proof, pending review, stale or failed evidence, blockers, prerequisites, and relevant schedule context.” |
| 6:30–6:50 | Point to decision form | “Readiness is calculated from deterministic rules. An authorized engineer must provide a reason and complete the required authorization step before recording a controlled decision. This creates a transparent and auditable approval boundary.” |
| 6:50–7:10 | Open **Turnover** | “Finally, once the required gate is approved, the system can generate a hash-verifiable turnover pack. Only accepted evidence and approved gate decisions can enter the handover package.” |
| 7:10–7:30 | Stay on Turnover or return to Overview | “Pramana Cx transforms commissioning from a fragmented document-driven process into a connected, evidence-backed, human-governed control system. It improves traceability, reduces review friction, and gives project teams a defensible path from controlled source to final turnover. Thank you.” |

## Strong final line

> “Pramana Cx does not replace engineering judgment—it gives engineering judgment a trustworthy, connected, and auditable foundation.”

## Delivery guidance

- Say **advisory** whenever showing AI-generated content.
- Say **deterministic** when discussing readiness, schedules, or numeric test verdicts.
- Do not claim live data if the interface labels it as simulated or synthetic.
- Keep cursor movement deliberate and pause briefly after each page change.
- If time is limited, prioritise Overview, Sources, Cx Tests, Schedule, Shipments, Readiness, and Turnover.
